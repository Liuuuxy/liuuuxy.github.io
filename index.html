About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Arizona State University, advised by Ransalu Senanayake. I work on information-efficient AI systems — how intelligent systems can make better decisions from limited but informative signals. That question runs through my work on LLM evaluation and routing, cognitive evaluation for humanoid robots, imitation learning for embodied agents, and multimodal learning for surgical and medical systems.

Before ASU I was a research assistant in the MAPLE Lab at Vanderbilt University, working with Jie Ying Wu and Nabil Simaan on multimodal models for surgical robotics. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Rice University and my B.S. in Software Engineering from Huazhong University of Science & Technology.

Physical Agentic AI: An Architecture for Orchestrating a Robot Crew with LLMs

Xinyuan Liu, Eren Sadikoglu, Riana Chatterjee, Ransalu Senanayake

arXiv:2608.22657, 2026

An LLM mission planner proposes a structured plan over typed skill registries but never actuates; a procedural orchestrator holds sole actuation authority and checks every step against declarative workflow contracts at dispatch time. Instantiated on a real Unitree G1 humanoid and Go2 quadruped, and on an air–ground search-and-rescue stack in Gazebo.
