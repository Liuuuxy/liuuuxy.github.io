PhD Student, Computer Science · Arizona State University · LENS Lab

Hi! I'm Xinyuan Liu, a second-year Computer Science PhD student at Arizona State University, advised by Prof. Ransalu Senanayake and affiliated with the Laboratory for Learning Evaluation and Naturalization of Systems (LENS Lab).

I work on information-efficient AI systems: AI that makes good decisions from limited but informative signals. Recently that has meant matching users to the right LLM from limited feedback, evaluating humanoid robots on cognitive and human-factors criteria rather than task completion alone, orchestrating robot teams with LLM planners whose every step is checked before it executes, and learning embodied skills from few demonstrations.

Before ASU, I was a research assistant in the MAPLE Lab at Vanderbilt University with Prof. Jie Ying Wu, building multimodal models for surgical robotics, and before that a software engineering intern at Ra Labs. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Rice University and my B.S. in Software Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

I'm glad to talk about research and possible collaborations — feel free to reach out by email.
