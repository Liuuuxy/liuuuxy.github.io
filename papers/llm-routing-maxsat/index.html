LLM Routing as Reasoning: A MaxSAT View

Son Nguyen, Xinyuan Liu, Ransalu Senanayake

ICLR 2026 Workshop on Logical Reasoning of Large Language Models

arXiv Workshop paper

Summary

Casts LLM routing under natural-language feedback as weighted MaxSAT, turning feedback into hard and soft constraints over model attributes, and studies routing with and without feedback on a 25-model benchmark.
