Physical Agentic AI: An Architecture for Orchestrating a Robot Crew with LLMs

Xinyuan Liu, Eren Sadikoglu, Riana Chatterjee, Ransalu Senanayake

arXiv preprint, 2026

Abridged version at the IROS 2026 Workshop on Foundation Models in Multi-Robot Systems.

arXiv Code Hardware demos

Summary

An LLM mission planner proposes a structured plan over typed skill registries but never actuates; a procedural orchestrator holds sole actuation authority and checks every step against declarative workflow contracts at dispatch time. Demonstrated on a real Unitree G1 humanoid and Go2 quadruped, and on an air–ground search-and-rescue stack in Gazebo.
