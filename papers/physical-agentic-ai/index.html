Physical Agentic AI: An Architecture for Orchestrating a Robot Crew with LLMs

Xinyuan Liu, Eren Sadikoglu, Riana Chatterjee, Ransalu Senanayake

arXiv preprint, 2026

Abridged version at the IROS 2026 Workshop on Foundation Models in Multi-Robot Systems.

arXiv Code Full demo clips

Summary

An LLM mission planner emits a structured plan over typed skill registries but never actuates. Declarative workflow contracts render the planner's prompt and also drive enforcement, so the two cannot drift apart, and a procedural orchestrator holds sole actuation authority: it checks every step against the contract at dispatch time and may trigger one feedback-driven replan. The framework is instantiated on a real Unitree G1 humanoid and Go2 quadruped doing pick-and-deliver, and on a simulated air–ground search-and-rescue team (PX4 quadrotor and TurtleBot3) in Gazebo.
